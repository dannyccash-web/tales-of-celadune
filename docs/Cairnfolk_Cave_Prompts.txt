TALES OF CELADUNE - CAIRNFOLK CAVE: CHARACTER PROMPTS
=====================================================
7 Cairnfolk: 3 men, 3 women, 1 girl (about 8).
Matched to Durgan Coldforge and Morrick Stillstone: grey granite skin in flaky,
layered strata with mica glints, heavy brows, broad stocky builds, hair and
beards that look carved from stone, muted weathered workwear.
Each prompt stands on its own. Copy everything between the lines.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BRANNOCH DEEPVEIN - elder engineer, head of the cave (male, ~300 years old)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

PORTRAIT:
Full-body fantasy RPG character portrait, painterly semi-realistic style, plain transparent background, vertical 2:3 framing. A very old Cairnfolk man, a stocky stone-skinned humanoid: skin of grey granite in flaking layered strata with tiny glinting mica flecks, heavy craggy brow, deep-set amber eyes. A long beard and eyebrows that look like pale weathered stone, cracked and chalky white, with thin quartz veins running through his skin. He is slightly hunched but still broad. He wears a long faded brown wool coat over a linen shirt, a leather tool belt holding brass calipers, a plumb bob and a folding rule, and round spectacles with polished quartz lenses. He holds a rolled parchment of engineering drawings under one arm and a stout walking staff of iron-shod oak. Expression: shrewd, patient, quietly proud. Muted earthy palette, warm soft lighting, same style as a weathered stone-folk blacksmith character.
